The Technology

How 222nm Far-UVC Works

Three reasons why 222nm Far-UVC is the most significant breakthrough in indoor air safety in a generation.

🦠

Destroys Airborne Pathogens

222nm photons shatter the protein shell and RNA of viruses and bacteria floating in the air — including COVID-19, influenza, and MRSA — in under seconds.

🛡️

Safe for Human Exposure

Unlike traditional 254nm UV, 222nm light is entirely absorbed by the dead outer layer of skin and the eye's tear film. It cannot reach living cells. Runs safely 24/7 with people present.

⚡

Mutation-Proof Defense

222nm Far-UVC attacks the fundamental physical structure of every virus — not surface proteins. No matter how a virus mutates, it cannot develop resistance to physics.

🔬

Peer-Reviewed & Proven

Validated by Columbia University, University of Hong Kong, HKUST, and leading global research institutions. Endorsed in a WHO webinar. Deployed across Hong Kong hospitals, airports, and schools.

Built at HKSTP. Trusted Globally.
