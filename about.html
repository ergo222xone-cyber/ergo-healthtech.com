Who We Are

ERGO HealthTech Ltd. is headquartered at the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks (HKSTP) in Pak Shek Kok, Sha Tin, Hong Kong. We are an all-rounded expert in human health protection, developing cutting-edge technology across automated spatial disinfection, Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) monitoring and management, and portable sterilization solutions.

Our mission is simple: to make every human-occupied space actively safer. Not passively safer — actively. We deploy 222nm Far-UVC germicidal technology that continuously neutralizes airborne pathogens while people are present, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Our Journey

WHO Endorsement

222nm Far-UVC technology endorsed in a WHO webinar in December 2021, establishing global scientific credibility for the wavelength.
